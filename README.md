# Pharma-Sales-Dashbord-
# Project Overview
1-Client: Pharma Company -Kipla
2-Purpose: Enable sales and Marketing team to analyze drug performance 
3-Scope: Sales trend, Regional Performance, Supplier, Revenue forecasting

# Business Goals
1-	Track how different drugs/product are performing across market 
2-	Understanding which regions or zones are doing well or underperforming 
3-	Identify top suppliers and flag inefficiencies in the supply chain.
4-	Support Sales planning and revenue forecasting using historical trends.
5-	Improve Accountability and performance across the commercial ecosystem(Sales, Supply Chain, Marketing).
6-	Automate the report refresh every day.

# What I Worked On
1- Data modeling with multiple linked tables
2- Business-focused KPIs and DAX measures
3- Interactive visuals for decision-making
4- End-to-end documentation covering business logic & process flow
